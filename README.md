# 🚨 Raksha Thunai (रक्षा துணை) — AI Emergency Intelligence

> **Next-Generation AI Emergency Response & Women's Safety System**
> Powered by Real-Time Acoustic Intelligence, Silent SOS Triggers, Multilingual AI Voice Assistant, and Automated Rescue Protocol.

---

## 🌟 Overview

**Raksha Thunai** is an ultra-reliable, AI-driven emergency response platform designed to protect individuals in high-risk situations. Combining low-latency voice AI, silent panic gesture recognition, ambient noise analysis, and rapid emergency dispatch coordination, Raksha Thunai ensures help is dispatched in seconds without alerting perpetrators.

---

## ✨ Key Features

- ⚡ **Instant SOS & Acoustic Threat Detection**: Analyzes distress keywords, scream pitch, and physical violence signatures in real time.
- 🤫 **Silent Tap Pad & Cancel Shield**: Concealed panic triggers on mobile screens with PIN-protected false-alarm cancellation.
- 🗣️ **Multilingual AI Voice Assistant**: Communicates fluently across multiple Indian languages (Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Bengali, English) for effortless emergency reporting.
- 🚁 **Automated Rescue Operations**: Real-time dispatch telemetry, live location broadcasting, and emergency contacts pinging.
- 🛡️ **Zero-Trust Architecture & Privacy First**: On-device encryption and GDPR/DPDP-compliant data handling during sensitive incidents.
- 📱 **Interactive React Simulation**: Full interactive simulator included in the landing page demonstrating real-time SOS lifecycle.
